Useful things to know.

Can I start as a beginner?

Yes. The Beginner filter shows starting points for kids, teens, and adults.

Are there classes for kids and adults?

Yes. Choose Kids, Teens, or Adults in Courses to see options for that age group.

What if I haven’t picked an instrument?

Explore guitar, keyboard, vocals, and drums in the learning path finder. Include music you like or an instrument you have access to in your enquiry.

How do I choose a class time?

Compare weekly sessions using day and time filters in Fees & Batches. Times are shown in IST.

Does an enrollment request reserve my place?

Your request is saved on this device. The academy needs to confirm a place before you are enrolled.

Where can I see fees?

Each batch shows an indicative fee and the period it covers. Review these details before you request enrollment.
