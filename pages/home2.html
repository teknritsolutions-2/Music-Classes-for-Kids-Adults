How music learning works

  1. Choose an instrument

    Pick the sound you want to learn.

  2. Join a suitable batch

    Match your level and a day you can attend.

  3. Learn and practice

    Try a new skill in class, then work on it between lessons.

  4. Build your skills

    Play longer pieces and learn to keep time with others.

Explore the learning stages
Start with the basics

Learn how to hold the instrument or warm up your voice. Play a few notes or tap a simple beat.

Learn with an instructor

Work through one chord change, rhythm, or short passage and try the feedback straight away.

Practice your skills

Slow down the part that needs work. Repeat it at a comfortable speed before moving on.

Play with others

Count in together and listen for the other parts so everyone can stay in time.

Build confidence

Share a piece when you feel ready. A run-through or recording can help you hear your progress.

Inside a music lesson

  1. Warm up. Get comfortable with the instrument and find a steady beat.
  2. Try one technique. Work on a chord, a rhythm, or part of a song with your teacher.
  3. Play it again. Use feedback to decide what to practice at home. In a group, listen for how your part fits.

Questions before you join?

Can I start with no experience?

Yes. Select Beginner in the class finder and choose the age group and day that suit you.

What if I’m deciding between two instruments?

Compare the skills taught in each course, or ask an instructor for a starting point based on the music you like.

Does saving an enrollment request reserve a place?

The request is saved on this device. The academy confirms availability and fees separately.
