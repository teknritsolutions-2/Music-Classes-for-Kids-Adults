A lesson at UPBEAT

From warm-up to a song.

  1. Warm up

    Get comfortable with your instrument and find a steady pulse.

  2. Learn a technique

    Try one change at a time with a teacher’s guidance.

  3. Practice and get feedback

    Repeat a short part, listen back, and make an adjustment.

  4. Play it in context

    Bring the part back into a piece of music.

Student progress

Build on what you know.

Progress can look different for every learner. These are useful milestones to work toward.

  1. Play a short part

    Build a pattern or phrase you can play at a comfortable pace.

  2. Connect the parts

    Play longer passages with steady timing and fewer stops.

  3. Listen and respond

    Hold your part while playing with an instructor or a group.
