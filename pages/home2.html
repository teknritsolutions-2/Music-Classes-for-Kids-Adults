The next steps

First lesson to first performance.

  1. Explore

    Pick an instrument and a piece you’d like to play.

  2. Learn

    Build the technique you need one part at a time.

  3. Practice

    Slow down tricky passages and keep a steady beat.

  4. Collaborate

    Try your part alongside other musicians.

  5. Perform

    Prepare a piece to share when you feel ready.
