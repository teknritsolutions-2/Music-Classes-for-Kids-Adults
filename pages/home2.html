Inside a typical lesson

  1. Warm up. Get comfortable with the instrument and find a steady beat.
  2. Try one technique. Work on a chord, a rhythm, or part of a song with your teacher.
  3. Play it again. Use the feedback to decide what to practice at home.

Your learning journey

Explore what you can work on from your first lesson to playing with others.

Start with the basics

Learn how to hold the instrument or warm up your voice. Play a few notes or tap a simple beat.

Learn with an instructor

Work through one chord change, rhythm, or short passage and try the feedback straight away.

Practice your skills

Slow down the part that needs work. Repeat it at a comfortable speed before moving on.

Play with others

Count in together and listen for the other parts so everyone can stay in time.

Build confidence

Share a piece when you feel ready. A run-through or recording can help you hear your progress.
