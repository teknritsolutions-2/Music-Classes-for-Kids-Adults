The learning journey

Build it one beat at a time.

  1. Explore

    Start with a sound or song you want to play.

  2. Learn

    Break the technique into manageable parts.

  3. Practice

    Slow down tricky passages and find a steady beat.

  4. Collaborate

    Learn to listen and play your part alongside others.

  5. Perform

    Share a piece when you feel ready.
