How we teach

Learn by making music.

One clear goal at a time gives learners something they can hear and build on.

Start with a sound you like
Explore guitar, keyboard, vocals, or drums through music you’re curious about.
Practice with guidance
Work on one change at a time so you can hear what difference it makes.
Get comfortable playing with others
Listen, count in, and find your place in a group.

Practice to performance

Follow the song.

  1. Try a short part

    Choose a few bars you can play comfortably.

  2. Bring it together

    Try the part with an instructor or another learner.

  3. Share it when you’re ready

    Work toward performing if it’s a goal you want to pursue.
