About UPBEAT

UPBEAT Music Academy offers guitar, keyboard, vocals, and drum lessons for kids, teens, and adults. You can start as a beginner or build on what you already play.

How lessons work

Bring a song you like or ask your instructor to suggest a piece for your level.

Start with a short exercise
Use a few notes, a chord change, or a rhythm to work on one skill at a time.
Apply it to a piece of music
Play a short passage, listen to what changed, and use feedback to improve the next try.
Practice with other musicians
When you’re ready for group playing, learn to count in and follow the other parts.

Space to listen, try, and play

A lesson needs room to concentrate and hear the detail in a note. In rehearsal, the same ear helps you follow the rest of the group.

Individual instruction focuses on a part you’re working through. Group practice gives you a chance to keep time and respond to other players.

From practice to performance

A piece starts small. The class can help you bring it together one part at a time.

Build the part
Practice the notes and timing at a comfortable speed.
Play it with others
Plan the count-in and the changes you need to hear.
Share it when you’re ready
Play for others or record a run-through and listen back.
