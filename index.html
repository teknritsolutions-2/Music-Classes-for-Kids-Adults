Why learn at UPBEAT?

  • Start with the basics. Begin with a rhythm, chord, or short melody at your pace.
  • Get help with practice. Work through a tricky part and leave with something to try at home.
  • Find a class for your age. Kids, teens, and adults can choose a starting level.
  • Play with others. Learn to count in together and listen for the other parts.
