# Fine rhythm artwork

The new original pixel-rhythm-fine.svg uses short rounded capsule marks in the existing ultraviolet and lilac palette. Fixed rhythm bands vary opacity gently across the field. Medium and subtle surfaces use this artwork at a controlled scale with directional fading. Home 1’s prominent mosaic and authentication backgrounds retain their existing artwork.

The refined pattern appears in the Courses introduction, Home 2 finder heading, Instructors introduction, About, Contact, and Fees & Batches introductions. Selected closing invitations carry the same medium accent. Form controls, results, schedules, and photographs have no pattern overlay.

Brave captured 56 full-page combinations across seven routes, 390/768/1024/1440 px, light LTR and dark RTL. Actual full-page and detail images were inspected for scale, contrast, photographs, and contained placement. All combinations passed overflow and placement checks. Navigation, image fallback, disclosures, enquiry draft download, account controls, mobile drawers, and preference persistence also passed. Existing page content and handlers were retained.
